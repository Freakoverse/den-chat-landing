Why DEN Chat?

The platforms you rely on today have a fundamental problem: you don't own anything.

Censorship & Bans
Platform-wide account bans, message deletions, and content restrictions decided by corporations, not you.
Community Wipes
Entire servers and communities deleted overnight. Years of conversations, gone with no recourse or appeal.
No Privacy
Messages stored in plaintext on corporate servers. Readable by staff, bots, and anyone who gains access.
Permissioned Access
KYC, age verification, phone numbers, email confirmation. Gatekeeping who can even start a conversation.
Self-Hosting Fragmentation
Join a friend's server? Make an account. Another friend? Another account. UX is broken across instances.
Same Problems, Smaller Scale
Even self-hosted alternatives face the same censorship and control issues, scoped to whoever runs the instance.
DEN Chat was built to solve all of this.
  • Censorship-resistant
  • Permissionless
  • Encrypted by default
  • No KYC or age verification
  • One account everywhere
  • You own your identity

Reality Check

We believe in transparency. Here's what you should know.

Early Technology

This is new technology. DEN Chat is an experimental implementation of a specification that is not yet standardized or widely tested. The app is in alpha. Things will most likely break, features may not work as expected, and data could be lost. There will be a learning curve, especially if you're new to decentralized systems.

Freedom = Responsibility

With the highest freedom comes the highest responsibility. You are in full control, including managing your own security. Your seed phrase and private keys are yours alone. Losing or compromising them means no possible recovery. No one can help you. There is no "forgot password" button.
